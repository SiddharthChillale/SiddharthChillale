I'm a Senior Software Engineer at **IncubXperts** in Pune. My specialty is no one particular thing. I'm a generalist and I'm proud of it. Aside from the typical devlopment work in various languages, I have experience in **document extraction and parsing**: I take messy, unstructured documents and get LLMs to turn them into clean, structured data that downstream systems can trust. I also work on entity resolution and evals, and I write the AWS infrastructure all of it runs on.

My favorite part of the job is taking a "what if we tried..." notebook all the way to a feature real users rely on. I care about evals, clean infra, and not waking up to pager alerts.

After hours I keep building, mostly voice-first and local-first tools, because I'd rather listen to an article than read it on a tiny screen.

### 🔨 Things I've been building

- **[Read Aloud](https://github.com/SiddharthChillale/web-readaloud-extension)**: a browser extension for Chrome, Firefox, and Safari that reads articles aloud in natural Indian-English voices. It skips the ads and menus and highlights each sentence as it reads. It can also turn an article into a two-host podcast or a one-minute summary. Powered by Sarvam AI.
- **[slyfox](https://github.com/SiddharthChillale/slyfox)**: reads the terms & conditions so you don't have to. It flags the catches and links each one to the exact line it came from. You bring your own API key, and there's no server involved.
- **[vue2-vue3-migration-skills](https://github.com/SiddharthChillale/vue2-vue3-migration-skills)**: skills that teach coding agents to handle the tedious parts of a Vue 2 → 3 migration.
- **A personal morning news podcast**: a fully local pipeline that gathers the day's news, writes a script, voices it with Kokoro TTS, and drops an episode into my iCloud before coffee.
